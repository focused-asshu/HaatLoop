# Research templates

**NO research has been done yet.** All logs below are empty. These templates contain no findings, participants, quotes, questions, or sample data. The founder will add real interview guides later.

Evidence labels must remain distinct: a **hypothesis** is an untested proposition; an **observation** records what was directly seen with context; an **interview statement** is what a participant reported and is not independently verified; **experimental evidence** records a defined intervention, method, and measured result; a **verified outcome** requires a documented verification method and supporting source. Do not promote one category into another without evidence.

Use pseudonymous participant IDs and minimal personal data. Obtain appropriate consent and keep sensitive details out of the public repository.
